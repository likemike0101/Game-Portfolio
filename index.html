Bouncy Anchors (P2 Gold)

I created a 1v1 2d platforming shooting game played with controllers. It features an anchor that moves the player shooting it and getting hit by it. Use it to hit players off the stage and win.

Technology Used:

Unity, C#

My Contributions:

  • I created everything in the game with the exception of the controller button assets and the creation of the audio
  • The physics and function of the anchor is definitly a highlight of the game. From what happens to the player wehn they get hit to how it moves the player that shoots it
  • I also am proud of the game development and how it leads the player into figuring out what to do without directly telling them.
